\section*{Model}

Tick-counter coupled model under PDEVS:
\[
  C = \langle X_C,\, Y_C,\, D,\, \{M_d\},\, \text{EIC},\, \text{EOC},\, \text{IC} \rangle
\]
\[
  D = \{G_{1/10},\; G_{1},\; K\},\quad X_C = \emptyset,\quad Y_C \subseteq \mathbb{N}
\]
\[
  \text{IC} = \{(G_{1/10}.\texttt{out} \to K.\texttt{add}),\;
               (G_{1}.\texttt{out} \to K.\texttt{reset})\}
  \qquad
  \text{EOC} = \{(K.\texttt{sum} \to C.\texttt{out})\}
\]

Expected invariant: every output of $K$ equals $10$.

\subsection*{Port Types}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
  \toprule
  Model & Port & Type \\
  \midrule
  $G_{1/10}$ & \texttt{out}   & \texttt{out\_port<int>} \\
  $G_{1}$    & \texttt{out}   & \texttt{out\_port<reset\_tick>} \\
  $K$        & \texttt{add}   & \texttt{in\_port<int>} \\
             & \texttt{reset} & \texttt{in\_port<reset\_tick>} \\
             & \texttt{sum}   & \texttt{out\_port<int>} \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Distinct message types on the two generator outputs enforce the routing
contract at compile time.

\subsection*{Counter Transitions (PDEVS)}

At every integer second $G_{1/10}$ and $G_1$ are simultaneously imminent.
PDEVS delivers their outputs as a single bag; $K$ receives both
\texttt{add} and \texttt{reset} in the same external call:
\[
  \delta^K_{\text{ext}}\!\left(s,\, e,\,
    \langle \texttt{add}\!:[1],\; \texttt{reset}\!:[\cdot] \rangle\right)
  = (n + 1,\; \text{ready})
\]

The confluence function (internal-first) handles the case where $K$ is
also imminent:
\[
  \delta^K_{\text{con}}(s, e, x) =
    \delta^K_{\text{ext}}\!\left(\delta^K_{\text{int}}(s),\; 0,\; x\right)
\]
Internal transition fires first (clears ready, resets accumulator),
then the tick from $G_{1/10}$ is applied.  This is equivalent to
$G_{1/10} \prec G_1$ in ordering.

\section*{Implementation Notes}

\paragraph{Counter $K$.}
\texttt{cadmium::basic\_models::pdevs::accumulator<int,TIME>} is used
directly.

\paragraph{Generators.}
\texttt{tick\_gen<TIME>} and \texttt{reset\_gen<TIME>} are defined in the
experiment source.  \texttt{tick\_gen} returns period
\texttt{TIME\{1\}/TIME\{10\}}; \texttt{reset\_gen} returns \texttt{TIME\{1\}}.

\paragraph{Driver.}
Outputs are captured by driving \texttt{pdevs::coordinator} directly and
inspecting \texttt{outbox\_port<sum>} after each \texttt{collect\_outputs}
call (termination by reset count, not by float end-time).

\paragraph{TIME types exercised.} \texttt{float} only (see expectations below).

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section*{Expected Observations}

\textbf{Float time:} IEEE~754 cannot represent $1/10$ exactly.
Accumulated error over repeated addition of \texttt{0.1f} shifts occasional
$G_{1/10}$ ticks across a 1-second boundary; $K$ outputs $9$ or $11$.
Observed: ${\approx}11.4\%$ error rate over 100\,000 resets
(histogram: $9 \times 10\,406$, $10 \times 88\,624$, $11 \times 970$).
