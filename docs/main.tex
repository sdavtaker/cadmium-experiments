\subsection*{Simulation Results}
\label{sec:ckw10-ncs-results}

Table~\ref{tab:ckw10-ncs} reports the mean, minimum, and maximum MSE over
15 replications for each $\tau_{\max}$ value ($T=10\,000$\,s, $\Delta q=0.01$,
seed base~42).  MSE rises sharply between $\tau_{\max}=0.7$ and $0.8$\,s:
at $\tau_{\max}=0.8\,\mathrm{s}=h$ the sampled-data loop loses stability,
consistent with the PowerDEVS curves in CKW10~Figure~7.
